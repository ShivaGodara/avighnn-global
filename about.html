We started Avighnn Global to give international buyers a single, accountable route into India's aluminium extrusion capacity. We vet the plants, develop the dies, and carry the documentation — so the profile you specified is the profile that reaches your port, verified and on paper.

The model

A merchant exporter — not a plant, not a broker.

A plant sells you its own capacity. A broker forwards your inquiry and steps back. We sit in between, on your side: we choose the plant, own the drawing, control the documentation, and carry the shipment to your port.

That means one contract, one contact, and one party answerable for the profile, the paperwork, and the freight.

◇

We control the paperwork

MTCs, dimensional reports, and export docs on every lot.

Customs, loading, and delivery to your named port.

Why Avighnn

Six reasons buyers choose us.

Every claim here is a practice we run, not an outcome we promise. Hold us to them.

/ 01

Certified sourcing network

We produce only at ISO 9001-certified extrusion plants we have vetted, so the buyer inherits the audit instead of running one.

/ 02

Custom dies, without the friction

Send DWG, DXF, or STEP. We translate the drawing into a die, prototype it, and move it to production on your approval.

/ 03

Documentation with every lot

Mill test certificates, dimensional reports, and traceability travel with the goods — not on request, as standard.

/ 04

Export logistics, end-to-end

Customs clearance, export documentation, container loading, and port delivery are handled in-house, FOB or CIF.

/ 05

Flexible order quantities

Trial lots to full containers. We consolidate across profiles and alloys so smaller programmes still ship economically.

/ 06

One point of contact

One person owns your order from quote to port — no handoffs between sourcing, factory, and freight desks.

Where we are

Based in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh.

Meerut sits in India's northern industrial belt, within reach of extrusion capacity and the Delhi–NCR export corridor. From here we coordinate sourcing, inspection, and dispatch to the western and eastern ports.

We serve buyers across Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Africa.
