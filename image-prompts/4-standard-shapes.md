## THE SUBJECT — STANDARD SHAPES

Stock catalogue cross-sections sold with no die cost and no tooling wait: equal and
unequal L-angles, C and U channels, round tubes, square and rectangular tubes, flat
bars, square bars and solid rounds. Sizes such as 20 × 20 to 100 × 100 angle, Ø12 to
Ø100 round tube, 10 × 3 to 100 × 12 flat bar. Alloys 6061 / 6063 / 6082, usually
mill finish, lengths up to 6 m, sold by tonnage to fabricators and stockists.

**Important — this is NOT a window or curtain-wall image.** No glass, no gaskets, no
window frames, and no complex multi-chamber profiles. These sections are
deliberately **simple**: an L, a C, a circle, a square, a rectangle, a flat strip.
Simplicity and variety are the whole point.

**The shot:** a neat, deliberately ordered bundle of plain mill-finish aluminium
stock sections — one L-angle, one C-channel, one round tube, one square tube, one
rectangular tube and one flat bar — cut to roughly equal 250–300 mm lengths and
stacked or fanned so that **all six cut cross-section ends face the camera** in a
clean row or cluster. The appeal is inventory and variety: simple geometry,
repetition, consistent natural satin aluminium, honest mill finish with its faint
longitudinal extrusion lines. Tidy and warehouse-precise, like a stockist's sample
set laid out for a buyer. Keep every section the same natural mill silver — no
anodised colours, no black or bronze.

## STYLE

- **Medium:** photorealistic commercial product photography. Sharp, clean, high
  detail. Not illustration, not a 3D render look, not CGI-glossy, not AI-surreal.
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:2 landscape, 1536 × 1024.
- **Lighting:** soft, large diffused key light from the upper left with a subtle
  fill from the right. Gentle, controlled specular highlights running along the
  aluminium edges — aluminium reads as brushed satin metal, not chrome mirror. No
  harsh flash, no blown highlights, no heavy dramatic shadows.
- **Background:** seamless, very light cool neutral grey studio backdrop (around
  #F2F2F2 to #F7F7F7), gentle natural falloff, soft contact shadow under the parts.
  No clutter of any kind.
- **Composition:** the bundle angled roughly 30–40 degrees off axis so the cut ends
  face the camera — the cross-section is what a buyer recognises. Medium depth of
  field: the front of the subject is tack sharp, the far end falls off softly.
  **Keep the entire arrangement inside the frame with clear margin on all four
  sides — nothing may run off or bleed past an edge.**
- **Mood:** industrial precision, export-grade quality, quiet confidence. A German
  engineering catalogue, not a stock-photo factory floor.
- **No text of any kind** — no labels, logos, brand names, watermarks, dimension
  callouts, or numbers printed on the parts. Website text will sit over this image.
- **No people, no hands, no faces.**
