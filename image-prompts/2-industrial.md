## THE SUBJECT — INDUSTRIAL EXTRUSIONS

Functional extrusions for automation, tooling, and thermal management: modular
T-slot aluminium framing in 20, 30, 40 and 45 series with 6/8/10 mm slots, finned
extruded heat sinks for power electronics and LED drivers, enclosure and rack
framing with panel grooves, and machine-guard sections. Typical alloy 6061-T6 or
6063-T5, often supplied cut, drilled and tapped to drawing.

**Important — this is NOT a window or curtain-wall image.** No glass, no glazing
pockets, no rubber gaskets, no window frames. The subject is machine-building and
thermal hardware.

**The shot:** a compact arrangement combining the three signature products.

- A **40 × 40 mm T-slot extrusion** cut to roughly 250 mm, standing upright with its
  cross-section facing the camera: a square profile with a T-shaped slot centred on
  each of its four faces and a small round bore through the middle. The slots and
  bore must be clearly readable.
- A **finned heat-sink extrusion** — a solid flat base with a comb of tall, evenly
  spaced parallel fins rising from it, clear anodised — angled so the fin array
  catches the light and reads as a crisp repeating rhythm.
- A short piece of **rack or enclosure profile** with a panel groove, set behind or
  beside them.

Add one small technical cue: a couple of T-slot nuts and a hex-socket bolt resting
on the surface, and a drilled-and-tapped hole visible on one cut end. The parts may
rest on a dark navy matte surface for contrast.

## STYLE

- **Medium:** photorealistic commercial product photography. Sharp, clean, high
  detail. Not illustration, not a 3D render look, not CGI-glossy, not AI-surreal.
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:2 landscape, 1536 × 1024.
- **Lighting:** soft, large diffused key light from the upper left with a subtle
  fill from the right. Gentle, controlled specular highlights running along the
  aluminium edges — aluminium reads as brushed satin metal, not chrome mirror. No
  harsh flash, no blown highlights, no heavy dramatic shadows.
- **Background:** seamless, very light cool neutral grey studio backdrop (around
  #F2F2F2 to #F7F7F7), gentle natural falloff, soft contact shadow under the parts.
  No clutter of any kind.
- **Composition:** parts angled roughly 30–40 degrees off axis so the cut
  cross-section ends face the camera — the cross-section is what a buyer
  recognises. Medium depth of field: the front of the subject is tack sharp, the far
  end falls off softly. **Keep the entire arrangement inside the frame with clear
  margin on all four sides — nothing may run off or bleed past an edge.**
- **Mood:** industrial precision, export-grade quality, quiet confidence. A German
  engineering catalogue, not a stock-photo factory floor.
- **No text of any kind** — no labels, logos, brand names, watermarks, dimension
  callouts, or numbers printed on the parts. Website text will sit over this image.
- **No people, no hands, no faces.**
