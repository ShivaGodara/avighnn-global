How we work

From inquiry to port delivery.

One workflow, one point of contact, one accountable party. You send the spec; we run sourcing, sampling, production, documentation, and freight to your port — FOB or CIF. Here is exactly what happens, and when.

01
Inquiry

Send drawings or describe the application.

02

Priced within 72 to 96 hours, FOB or CIF.

03
Sample

Die trial and first-article approval.

04
Production

Run at ISO 9001 partner plants.

05
Shipping

Customs, docs, and container loading.

06
Delivery

Port delivery with full lot documentation.

01
Inquiry
Send a drawing (DWG / DXF / STEP) or describe the application, alloy, finish, quantity, and destination port. Not sure of the spec? Describe the use and we'll help you land on the right profile.
DWG / DXF / STEPor plain description
02
Quote
We confirm feasibility, alloy, and tooling, then price the order — including any die cost, unit price, and freight. Quotes land within 72 to 96 hours on FOB or CIF terms.
72–96 hoursFOB / CIFdie cost + unit + freight
03
Sample & approval
For custom sections we cut a die, run a trial, and correct it until it holds tolerance. You approve a first article — with a dimensional report — before the production run.
die trialcorrectionfirst-article report
04
Production
The lot runs at an ISO 9001-certified partner plant we've vetted. Extrusion, heat treatment (ageing), finishing, and cutting proceed to spec, with in-process checks.
ISO 9001 plantageing / finishin-process QC
05
Inspection & documentation
Before dispatch we compile the paperwork: mill test certificate, dimensional report, packing list, and export documents. Documentation travels with every lot — as standard, not on request.
MTCdimensional reportpacking listexport docs
06
Shipping & delivery
We handle bundling, container loading, customs clearance, and booking, then deliver to your port on the agreed incoterm. You get shipping documents and tracking through to arrival.
container loadingcustomsport delivery
